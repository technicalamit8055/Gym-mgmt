GYM NUTRITION APP ASSET PACK

The key reusable idea is the fixed meal-category system:
- breakfast.png
- lunch.png
- dinner.png
- snacks.png

Use those four fixed category icons/illustrations for every food item in that meal.
You do NOT need a separate generated icon for every food.

Also included:
- light/dark logos
- light/dark hero banners
- nutrition icons
- reusable meal illustrations
- empty states
- backgrounds
- progress/chart graphics
- plan, notification and QR graphics
- membership backgrounds

These are raster PNG crops from the generated asset sheet. For production,
use SVG for interface icons and WebP/AVIF for larger photographic illustrations.
